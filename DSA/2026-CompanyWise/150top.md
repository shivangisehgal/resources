## How topics were assigned

Topics come from LeetCode's official tags (via the neenza/leetcode-problems and doocs/leetcode datasets on GitHub). When a problem has several tags, one topic is picked by priority: Tries > Binary Search Trees > Graphs (graph, union-find, topological sort) > Linked Lists > Binary Trees > Dynamic Programming > BFS/DFS (Graphs) > Heaps > Sliding Window/Two Pointers > Binary Search > Stacks/Queues > Recursion/Backtracking > Bit Manipulation > Greedy > Strings > Arrays. About 30 classics were placed by hand where this rule misfires (e.g. Trapping Rain Water → Two Pointers, Jump Game → Greedy, Word Break → DP).
